Pro Scalper Approximation v3.7 — How to Read Signals, Exits & Stops

A visual breakdown of the strategy's logic: a Kalman-filtered SuperTrend engine that generates BUY/SELL signals, an ADX Volatility Wave zone model used as an exit reference, and a layered ATR bracket exit system with breakeven, zone-touch, and time stops.

1 · End-to-End Overview

Each bar closes → the engine recomputes the trend state and wave zones → a signal may fire → position is managed by four independent exit conditions until one triggers.

2 · Entry Signals (BUY / SELL)

Signals come only from a SuperTrend trend-state flip. Being overbought/oversold does not create an entry — those wave zones are for exits only.

Kalman filter

Smooths raw close with process noise Q=0.01 and measurement noise R=0.2. Feeds the smoothed estimate into the trend bands so flips are cleaner than raw-price SuperTrend.

SuperTrend core

Hand-built band recurrence with ATR period = 7, factor = 2.0. Trend state is +1 (long bias) or −1 (short bias); the plotted band follows the active side.

The actual trigger

flippedLong / flippedShort — the state must change this bar. One signal per flip, and they strictly alternate BUY → SELL → BUY.

Session gate

Only fires during 0930–1600. The trend keeps computing overnight, but flips outside RTH are suppressed (options aren't tradable then).

3 · Exit Logic — Four Independent Conditions

Once a position is open, whichever of these fires first closes the trade. They run every bar in parallel.

    P[Position OPEN] --> C1{Hard STOP hit?}
    C1 -- Yes --> X1(["Stop-loss exit
entry ∓ 1.5×ATR
(or breakeven if moved)"])
    C1 -- No --> C2{TARGET hit?}
    C2 -- Yes --> X2(["Profit-target exit

    C2 -- No --> C3{"Opposite WAVE
zone touched?"}
    C3 -- Yes --> X3(["Zone exit
OB Zone Exit / OS Zone Exit"])
    C3 -- No --> C4{"Held ≥ 12 bars?"}
    C4 -- Yes --> X4(["Time stop
close_all"])
    C4 -- No --> P

    class X1,X2,X3,X4 ex;
ExitTypeTriggerPurpose
Stop-lossProtective Price hits entry − 1.5×ATR (long) / entry + 1.5×ATR (short). ATR is frozen at entry. Caps the loss on a failed flip.
Profit targetTake profit Price hits entry ± 2.0×ATR. Gives a fixed 2:1.5 reward:risk bracket. Books the win without waiting for the opposite flip.
Zone-touch exitReference Long closes when price tags the upper wave outer edge (overbought); short closes at the lower edge (oversold). Toggle: useZoneExit. Exit into exhaustion, mimicking Pro Scalper's TP zones.
Time stopHousekeeping Neither stop nor target hit within maxBarsInTrade = 12 bars → close_all. Toggle: useTimeStop. Frees capital from stalled, going-nowhere trades.

4 · Stop Management & Breakeven ("Trailing")

Important: this is not a continuous trailing stop. It's a one-time breakeven jump — once you're far enough in profit, the stop snaps from its ATR distance up to the entry price and stays there.

flowchart TD
    E[Trade opens] --> INIT["Stop = entry ∓ 1.5×ATR
beTriggered = false"]
    INIT --> W{"Favorable move
≥ 1.0×ATR?"}
    W -- No --> W
    W -- "Yes (long: high ≥ entry+1×ATR)
(short: low ≤ entry−1×ATR)" --> BE["beTriggered = true
Stop JUMPS to entry price"]
    BE --> LOCK["Stop stays at breakeven
(does NOT keep trailing)"]
    LOCK --> DONE{"Stop or target
eventually hit"}
    DONE --> OUT([Position closed])

    classDef be fill:#2a2411,stroke:#d29922,color:#e6edf3;
    class BE,LOCK be;

Trigger distance

breakevenAtrMult = 1.0 — after price moves 1×ATR in your favor, downside risk on that trade becomes ~zero (minus slippage/commission).

Where it lands

Stop moves exactly to entryPrice. It never advances further, so remaining upside runs to the fixed 2.0×ATR target or a wave/time exit.

One-shot flag

beTriggered latches true for the life of the trade and resets only on the next entry. No re-trailing on pullbacks.

How to read it on the chart: the grey line is your Entry Price, the red line is the live Stop Loss (you'll see it snap up to the grey entry line the moment breakeven triggers), and the green line is the fixed Take Profit. On-open labels SL and TP print the initial bracket values.

5 · ADX Volatility Wave Zones (Exit Reference Only)

The red (upper) and green (lower) gradient ribbons are overbought/oversold zones. They feed the zone-touch exit and the visual TP markers — they never generate entries.

flowchart TD
    EQ["Equilibrium = VWMA(close, 50)"] --> WID
    BB["BB stdev × 1.5"] --> WID
    ADX["ADX (len 14)"] --> WID
    WID["width = BBstdev × (1 + 0.8×ADX/100)
then RMA-smoothed"] --> OFF
    OFF["Inner edge = eq ± 1.0×width
Outer edge = eq ± 2.0×width"] --> ZONE
    ZONE{Price vs
outer edge} -- "≥ upper outer" --> OB["OVERBOUGHT
→ closes a LONG"]
    ZONE -- "≤ lower outer" --> OS["OVERSOLD
→ closes a SHORT"]
    ZONE -- "between" --> NEU["Neutral"]
    OB --> TP1["TP marker (long):
lower high while overbought"]
    OS --> TP2["TP marker (short):
higher low while oversold"]

    classDef z fill:#1e1630,stroke:#a371f7,color:#e6edf3;
    class OB,OS,NEU z;

Gradient ribbon

5 concentric fill layers per side; opacity rises toward the outer edge so "deeper into the zone" reads as more extreme.

Cooldown state

A wave marks "active" once price closes past its outer edge, and won't re-fire until price sits in the neutral band for 5 bars — prevents repeated same-zone triggers.

Compression

Yellow background = wave width near its own recent low → a fresh volatility wave is "due". Reference only; not wired into entries or exits.
