1. Core Concept — The Three Zones

The indicator divides every impulse move into three Fibonacci-derived zones, each with a distinct role in price action:

Golden Pocket (50%–61.8%)

Role: Entry / Reversal Zone

  • Where pullbacks terminate in a trending market
  • Highest probability entry zone (~65-70% hit rate)
  • Tightest stop loss placement (just below 61.8%)
  • Best risk/reward ratio (typically 2:1 to 4:1)

Decision Zone (150%–170%)

Role: Continuation / Profit Target

  • First significant resistance/support after continuation
  • Market decides: normal move or extended move
  • Partial profit-taking area
  • Break through → acceleration to Extreme Zone

Extreme Zone (238.2%–261.8%)

Role: Exhaustion / Counter-Trend Zone

  • Move is considered overextended here
  • High probability of reversal or significant pullback
  • Final profit target for trend trades
  • Counter-trend entry with divergence confirmation

3. Multi-Swing Confluence (SH/SL Stacking)

The most powerful feature of this indicator: it computes Fibonacci zones from two independent swing pairs and highlights where they overlap. Different swing ranges produce different fib levels — when they converge at the same price, the market is structurally agreeing on that level from multiple fractal perspectives.

Fig 3. — Two independent swing ranges produce Golden Pockets at slightly different levels. Where they overlap = structurally significant zone.

Why Multi-Swing Works Markets are fractal — each swing creates its own set of Fibonacci relationships. When levels from different swing cycles cluster at the same price, it means the market has multiple independent reasons to react there. This is true structural confluence, not just one indicator agreeing with itself.

4. Confluence Scoring System (0–7)

Each zone is scored from 0 to 7 based on how many independent technical factors align with it. Higher score = higher probability trade.

#FactorLogic at Golden PocketLogic at Decision ZoneLogic at Extreme Zone
1 VWAP VWAP ± ATR×tol from zone mid Same Same
2 EMA 9/21 Either EMA near zone mid Same Same
3 EMA 50 EMA 50 near zone mid Same Same
4 PDH / PDL / PDC Any prev-day level near zone Same Same
5 RSI Oversold (<35 bull) Mid-range trending (55-75) Overbought (>70 bull)
6 Volume Spike Vol > 1.5× SMA(20) Same Same
7 2nd Swing Fib Secondary fib level clusters Same Same

Score Interpretation

5-7: Very high confluence — primary setups, full position size
3-4: Moderate confluence — valid setups, reduced position
0-2: Low confluence — zone exists but no confirmation, skip or paper-trade
Important: RSI Scoring is Direction-Aware At the Golden Pocket, RSI oversold scores a point (bullish = price is washed out at support → good entry). At the Extreme Zone, RSI overbought scores a point (confirms exhaustion → good exit/fade). The RSI logic is different per zone because the purpose of each zone is different.

8. Alert Setup & Webhook Format

The indicator fires JSON-formatted alerts when price enters a zone with sufficient confluence. Compatible with TradingView webhooks, app notifications, and third-party automation.

Alert JSON Schema

{
  "ticker":      "SPX",
  "zone":        "GOLDEN_POCKET",   // or DECISION_ZONE, EXTREME_ZONE
  "price":       7394.50,
  "lo":          7389.76,            // zone lower boundary
  "hi":          7396.31,            // zone upper boundary
  "confluence":  5                   // score 0-7
}

TradingView Setup Steps

  1. Add the indicator to your chart
  2. Right-click chart → "Add Alert..."
  3. Condition: select "Trend Fib Confluence [MTF]"
  4. Choose "Any alert() function call"
  5. For webhooks: paste your endpoint URL and check "Webhook URL"
  6. Message: use {{message}} to forward the JSON payload

9. Common Pitfalls & Filters

❌ Trading Fib Levels in Chop

Fib zones are unreliable on range-bound, low-volume days. If ADX < 20 and no clear impulse move, skip.

Fix: Require a minimum impulse range (e.g., pRange > 2× ATR) before trusting zones.

❌ Wrong Swing Anchor

Auto-detection may pick minor swings instead of the significant move. Results in tight, irrelevant zones.

Fix: Increase swingLen from 5 to 8-10, or manually set trend direction.

❌ Entering Without Confirmation

Price touching a zone ≠ reversal. Many zones get swept before holding.

Fix: Wait for a reversal candle (hammer, engulfing) + close BACK INSIDE the zone. Don't front-run.

❌ Ignoring the Score

Trading every zone regardless of confluence leads to overtrading and low win rate.

Fix: Minimum score of 3/7 for entries. For scalping, prefer 4+. Save 5+ for full-size positions.

❌ No Stop Loss Discipline

"It'll come back" thinking at a broken Golden Pocket leads to large drawdowns.

Fix: Hard stop below 61.8% for GP entries. If broken, the setup is INVALID. Accept the loss and move on.

❌ Over-leveraging at EZ Fades

Extreme Zone fades look tempting but have lower win rate (~55%). Sizing too big = account risk.

Fix: Half size on counter-trend EZ fades. Only full size if RSI divergence + volume climax + score 5+.
